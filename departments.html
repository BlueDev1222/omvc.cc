OPSLAW ENFORCEMENT

Ottawa Police Service

The Ottawa Police Service is responsible for maintaining law and order within the city, enforcing municipal laws, investigating crimes, and responding to emergencies. They work closely with the community to prevent crime, protect citizens, and ensure public safety, while maintaining trust and accountability through professional policing standards.

OPPLAW ENFORCEMENT

Ontario Provincial Police

The Ontario Provincial Police provides provincial-wide policing, supporting municipalities with law enforcement, major incident response, and investigative services. They oversee traffic and highway safety, conduct criminal investigations, and collaborate with local and federal agencies to maintain public security across Ontario.

MTOTRANSPORTATION

Ontario Ministry of Transportation

The Ministry of Transportation oversees the planning, regulation, and maintenance of Ontario’s transportation infrastructure. This includes road safety enforcement, vehicle inspections, highway management, and ensuring efficient and safe travel for residents, commuters, and commercial traffic throughout the province.

RCMPLAW ENFORCEMENT

Royal Canadian Mounted Police

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police serves as Canada’s federal law enforcement agency, tasked with national security, organized crime investigations, and federal law enforcement. They provide support to provincial and municipal police, handle high-profile investigations, and ensure the enforcement of federal laws and protection of Canadian citizens.

Ottawa Fire Service

The Ottawa Fire Service provides comprehensive fire and emergency services, including fire suppression, rescue operations, emergency medical support, and hazardous materials response. They also focus on public education, fire prevention, and community outreach to protect lives, property, and the environment within Ottawa.
